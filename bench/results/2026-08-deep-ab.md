# Deep search A/B, 2026-08-25

The question the deep mode had to answer: does multi-agent orchestration
(plan, iterative search, write, verify with routed repair) beat the
single-shot pipeline where it should shine? Arms ran over the two hardest
strata of golden_v3: S5 multi-hop and S2 paraphrase, 64 questions, through
the live stack (bge-m3, bge-reranker-v2-m3, qwen3:4b-instruct), scored on
the sources of the final answer. Runs recorded in MLflow (experiment
`retrieval`, runs `ab-standard` and `ab-deep`).

## Results

| Arm | hit@8 | hit@5 | MRR | s/question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| standard (single shot) | **61/64 (95 %)** | n/r | **0.681** | **13.6** |
| deep (multi-agent) | 60/64 (94 %) | 55/64 (86 %) | 0.633 | 86 |

n/r: not retained in the standard arm's filtered log; its per-arm misses
were lost the same way and the miss overlap between arms is unknown.

## Verdict

**The deep mode does not improve retrieval on this corpus**: one more
miss, MRR down 0.048, at 6.3 times the latency. The verifier's repair
loops did not recover the hard cases; they mostly re-ranked around the
same evidence, slower.

Plausible causes, explicitly hypotheses until measured:
- The deep searcher merges per-sub-question pools and reranks to k=10,
  while the standard pipeline reranks a 40-candidate pool to k=8: the
  standard arm simply sees more candidates per query.
- The strict 4b verifier burns its budgets on rewording rather than on
  genuinely new evidence (observed live: full budgets on most multi-hop
  questions).

## Consequences

- The deep mode stays what it already is in the UI: an explicit, visibly
  slower opt-in. Its measured value is NOT recall; what it adds is claim
  verification against the cited passages, visible steps, and the strict
  abstention path. The product copy must not promise better answers.
- The two levers worth trying before any re-claim: give the deep searcher
  the same candidate depth as the standard pipeline, and calibrate the
  verifier so a repair round must introduce NEW evidence to continue.
