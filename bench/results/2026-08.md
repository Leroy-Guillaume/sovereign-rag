## Cross-lingual stratum (S4)

MRR 0.84 with `bge-m3`. Measured during the embedding upgrade on the
119-question v2 set: hit@8 100/119 -> 114/119 overall, MRR 0.717 -> 0.784,
and S4 (question in one language, target document in another) moved from
MRR 0.15 under `intfloat/multilingual-e5-small` (384d) to 0.84. S4 was the
stratum no fusion or reranker change had moved; the embedding model was the
bottleneck.

## Abstention (16 no-answer questions, stratum S6)

The scripted trilingual refusal regex credits 10/16. Manual review of the 6
transcripts it flagged: 5 are refusals phrased outside the pattern (for
example "L'article 150 du RGPD n'est pas mentionné dans les contextes
fournis"), 1 is ambiguous (a DE response that only described what the corpus
covers, without an explicit refusal), 0 answered from invented content.

Net result: 15/16 clean refusals, 1 ambiguous, 0 fabrications.
